### [White House Launches &\#x27;Super Intelligence Force&\#x27; Task Force with 120-Day Risk Report Mandate](https://www.wsj.com/tech/ai/new-ai-task-force-to-report-on-risks-of-technology-after-public-and-industry-concerns-b6308bef) ⭐️ 7.0/10

The White House established a new task force called the &\#x27;Super Intelligence Force&\#x27; to assess AI risks and define the federal government&\#x27;s role in responding to the technology, with a mandate to deliver a report within 120 days. The task force is led by Jay Clayton, whom the source identifies as the National Intelligence Director, though this attribution is questionable since Clayton is publicly known as the former SEC Chair rather than an intelligence official. President Trump has declined to impose new AI regulations, instead favoring a voluntary framework that includes external safety audits and stronger internal controls, while emphasizing that the US &\#x27;leads by a lot&\#x27; in AI and will maintain that advantage over China.

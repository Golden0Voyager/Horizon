**「Background」** Terence Tao, a Fields Medalist and one of the most respected mathematicians, presented his &\#x27;Math 2.0&\#x27; framework at Caltech, describing how AI is fundamentally reshaping mathematical research. The presentation outlines a paradigm shift in how mathematicians approach problem-solving. Community discussion on Hacker News reveals reported real-world consequences: researchers describing how AI-generated solutions have rendered years of their work obsolete, forcing them to rewrite grant requests and grapple with the emotional and professional implications of losing problems they had been working on for years.

**「Impact on mathematical research practice」** Mathematicians report that AI-generated solutions are quickly resolving open problems they had pursued for years, forcing them to scrap or rewrite research plans and grant applications and causing feelings of professional loss.

**「Community Discussion」** Commenters challenged Tao’s medical‑AI example, noting that many effective drugs and even general anesthesia lack fully understood mechanisms, and argued that clinicians would not hesitate to use an AI‑designed cancer treatment if it improved outcomes. Others echoed the emotional toll of seeing years of work rendered obsolete by AI solutions.

### [Terence Tao outlines AI-driven &\#x27;Math 2.0&\#x27; vision for mathematical research](https://teorth.github.io/tao-web/slides/math-2-0-caltech-2026.pdf) ⭐️ 7.0/10

Terence Tao presented his &quot;Math 2.0&quot; vision at Caltech in October 2026, describing how AI is fundamentally reshaping mathematical research. He noted that AI systems are now producing solutions to long-standing open problems, prompting mathematicians to rewrite grant proposals and experience emotional loss akin to losing an old friend. The talk also raised philosophical questions about whether understanding or mere utility should be valued in AI-generated knowledge.

hackernews · Anon84 · 10月11日 13:16 · [社区讨论](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50043026)

**「背景」** 陶哲轩（Terence Tao）是菲尔兹奖得主，被公认为当代最杰出的数学家之一。他在加州理工学院发表的&\#x27;Math 2.0&\#x27;演讲，阐述了他对人工智能如何从根本上重塑数学研究范式的看法。

**「影响」** 数学家们报告称，AI 已能在短时间内解决他们多年研究的开放问题，迫使他们重写刚刚提交的研究申请，并感到像失去老友一样的情感冲击。

**「Community Discussion」** Several commenters from drug discovery argued that Tao&\#x27;s slide on cancer medicine was poorly informed, pointing out that many effective drugs lack understood mechanisms and clinicians would not hesitate to prescribe an AI-designed cure. Meanwhile, mathematicians described the personal impact of AI, reporting that AI-generated solutions had ended years of work, forced them to rewrite grant requests, and evoked feelings of loss comparable to losing a lover or old friend.

### [韩国股市失宠，外资年内撤离超 8700 亿元](https://m.gelonghui.com/live/2705107) ⭐️ 7.0/10

韩国综合指数（KOSPI）下半年下跌约 22%，成为表现最差的主要指数之一；外国投资者年内已净撤出韩国股市约 1310 亿美元（约合 8767 亿元人民币），市场成交额较 5 月底峰值暴跌 70%。

telegram · zaihuapd · 10月11日 12:13

**「背景」** 韩国股市上半年曾是全球表现最好的主要市场之一，主要受益于 AI 半导体需求推动三星电子和 SK 海力士股价上涨，但外资在指数创新高期间已开始净卖出。存储芯片行业具有周期性特征，当需求前景出现不确定性时，市场高度集中于这两家公司的结构放大了下跌风险。

**「三星外资持股创 18 年新低」** 三星电子外资持股比例降至 46.38%，创 2008 年全球金融危机以来近 18 年最低水平，反映海外资金对韩国 AI 行情的信心正在减弱。
